    \caption{High-level overview of the CSDI--EDM denoiser ($\texttt{CSDIModel}$).
    From the noise level $\sigma$, \texttt{\_edm\_precond} yields the preconditioning
    scalars $c_{\mathrm{skip}}, c_{\mathrm{out}}, c_{\mathrm{in}}, c_{\mathrm{noise}}$.
    The $c_{\mathrm{in}}$-scaled target is assembled with the clean conditioning
    channels by \texttt{make\_diff\_input}, side information is built by
    \texttt{get\_side\_info}, and $c_{\mathrm{noise}}\,(B,)$ is mapped to
    $(B,\mathrm{demb\_dim})$ by \texttt{DiffusionEmbedding}. The \texttt{diff\_CSDI}
    backbone projects the two-channel input, processes it through $n$ stacked
    residual blocks with skip connections (positional encodings,
    \texttt{diffusion\_projection} and \texttt{cond\_projection} are internal to each
    block), and reduces to $F_x$. The denoised estimate is
    $D_x = c_{\mathrm{skip}}\,x_t + c_{\mathrm{out}}\,F_x$.}
