\subsection{Aggregate Statistics}
In this section the aggregate statistics for Sec.\ \ref{sec:phase1_results} are displayed in full in Tab.\ \ref{tab:phase1_raw_with_reference}. The configurations shared by all runs are: ODE sampler, $n$-windows $= 512$,
sequence length $= 2048$, stride $= 400$, diffusion steps $= 2000$,
W-count $= 1,048,576$. In this case reference statistics are computed on the windows generated for training from the trainig dataset, meaning that due to \(\texttt{stride}\) being smaller than \(L\) the total number of values is higher than the dataset one, due to repetition. The number of reference data is \(n=10,833,920\) or \(5290\) windows of \(L=2048\).
