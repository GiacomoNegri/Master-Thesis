\section{Baseline Models --- GARCH-X and Merton-X}
\label{sec:app_baselines}
Full distributional statistics for the GARCH-X and Merton-X baseline models alongside
the training reference are reported in Tab.\ \ref{tab:baseline_full}. GARCH-X was
evaluated on $n = 50{,}000{,}000$ generated samples; Merton-X likewise. From this pool a
10M-observation subsample was used for the aggregate statistics reported in the main
chapter (Tab.\ \ref{tab:baseline_aggregate}). The reference
comprises $n = 2{,}623{,}489$ observations from the training dataset (see
Sec.\ \ref{sec:ch4_data}).

\textbf{Aggregate Statistics} \quad
Due to the generation constraints that have been applied to GARCH-X and Merton-X, it was possible to bound the number of unlikely returns, i.e.\ returns whose absolute value exceeds one, \(|r_t|>1\), to approximately 0\% for GARCH-X and to 0.02\% for Merton-X (up to 4 decimal places); those few extreme generated values contribute to most of the kurtosis and skewness shifts.

\subsection{Aggregate Statistics}
In this section the aggregate statistics for Sec.\ \ref{sec:phase1_results} are displayed in full in Tab.\ \ref{tab:phase1_raw_with_reference}. The configurations shared by all runs are: ODE sampler, $n$-windows $= 512$,
sequence length $= 2048$, stride $= 400$, diffusion steps $= 2000$,
W-count $= 1,048,576$. In this case reference statistics are computed on the windows generated for training from the training dataset, meaning that, since \(\texttt{stride}\) is smaller than \(L\), the total number of values is higher than the dataset one, due to repetition. The number of reference data is \(n=10,833,920\) or \(5290\) windows of \(L=2048\).

Images of all the marginal distribution metrics and analysis are displayed in Fig.\ \ref{fig:app_phase1_marginal_ve} for VE SDE, Fig.\ \ref{fig:app_phase1_marginal_gbm} for GBM-style SDE, and Fig.\ \ref{fig:app_phase1_marginal_vp} for VP SDE.
