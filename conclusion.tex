\chapter{Conclusion}
In conclusion this thesis navigated the complexity of financial time series modeling discussing their properties and characteristic and tring to modeling these complex statistical distribution through deep learning models.

The thesis commenced by creating a baseline with known models GARCH-X and Merton-X. Subsequently, deep learning model were investigated, specifically the CSDI-based architecture proposed by Kim et al.\ \cite{kim2025diffusion}. This approach has shown some clear limitations in its ability to replicate the stylized facts and the heavy tailed distribution of such financial time series.

Introducing then the main contribution of this thesis, while clarifying Kim et al.\ \cite{kim2025diffusion} structure, it was expanded into the EDM framework. This conditioning structure provide the sufficient amount of information necessary to discern the intricate reletionship between volatility and extreme market events.

In conclusion while still further exploration and validation are required, specifically in real world application, our framework revealed to be functioning in modeling financial time series.
